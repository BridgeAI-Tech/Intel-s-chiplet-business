Leverage Robust Intel Foundry Design Ecosystem Options
Explore the world-class capabilities from our ecosystem partners, tuned to Intel Foundry technology, to turn your innovation into silicon.
Access a Wide Range of Design Capabilities
The Intel Foundry Accelerator Ecosystem Alliance Program has collaborated with ecosystem domain leaders to equip you with industry-leading electronic design automation tools (EDA), reusable design blocks (IPs), project acceleration and augmentation services, and design implementation environments to meet your unique needs.
IP Alliance
Intellectual Property Alliance is built on a comprehensive, silicon-verified, and Intel process-specific IP portfolio including standard cell libraries, embedded memories, general purpose I/O’s, analog IP, and interface IP.
EDA Alliance
Electronic Design Automation Alliance offers powerful and validated solutions optimized for Intel’s technology and manufacturing, covering the full spectrum from concept to high-volume silicon production.
Design Services Alliance
Allows you to focus on creating unique product ideas, assigning implementation tasks to rigorously trained designers well-versed in ecosystem technologies.
Cloud Alliance
Composed of leading cloud service providers and EDA partners, offering co-optimized solutions to enable design-on-the-cloud.
USMAG Alliance
United States Military, Aerospace, and Government Alliance enables national security and government applications.
Value Chain Alliance
Value Chain Alliance (VCA) is a network of ASIC design companies for specification-to-silicon solutions. VCA partners provide expertise across different applications, market segments and geographies.
Chiplet Alliance
The Chiplet Alliance focuses on optimizing the ecosystem to drive interoperable and secure chiplet implementations across a wide range of government and commercial applications.
Global Manufacturing
Benefit from a robust, geo-diverse, and expanding supply for wafers and assembly & test.
Process Technologies
Design your next generation of leadership products with our breakthrough technologies Intel 18A, Intel 3, and Intel 16.
